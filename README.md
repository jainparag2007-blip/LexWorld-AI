# LexWorld AI

AI-powered global legal literacy platform.

## What It Does
LexWorld AI makes laws and legal rights from every 
country instantly understandable — in plain language, 
in 30+ languages, for anyone with a smartphone.

## Problem
4 billion people cannot understand the laws that 
govern their lives.

## Solution
- AI Legal Assistant for any country
- Country Law Explorer — 195 countries
- Traveler Safety Guide
- Emergency Rights Mode
- 30+ Languages

## Technology
- React frontend
- Claude AI API by Anthropic
- Multilingual NLP support
